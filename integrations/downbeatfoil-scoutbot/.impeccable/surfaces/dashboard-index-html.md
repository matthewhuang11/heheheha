## Scope

Responder dashboard (`dashboard/index.html`), single file served by the robot. Mode: operate. Audience: HackGT judges at an expo table over a laptop, ~3-minute demo. Web fonts allowed, self-hosted.

## Direction contract

THESIS: a quiet dark console with one loud mission status; refuses the dense many-readout ops wall (and the rejected beacon skin).

OWN-WORLD: near-black panels, one state color at a time (violet searching, amber returning, orange syncing, green done), orange accent reserved for survivors, Archivo throughout.

STORY: judges see the robot go dark and search on its own, then watch survivors arrive and their photos develop as HQ triages them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: at 1440x900, no scroll. Full-width status banner with headline, sentence, clock, stop. Video fills the left; survivor cards on the right over a small map; mission buttons and drive keys under the video; one muted details line.

FORM: user-rejected the assigned rescue-beacon world (seed b15fd4ce) as too busy; rebuilt as the category standard played with restraint at the user's request. Signature interaction: survivor photo develops on triage. Motion grammar: expo ease-out crossfades, radar sweep while searching, card arrival, map glide.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
